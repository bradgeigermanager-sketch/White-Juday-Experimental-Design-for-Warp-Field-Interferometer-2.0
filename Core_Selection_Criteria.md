📋 Core selection criteria
The surface accuracy of all optical elements must be better than λ/8@633nm to avoid introducing additional wavefront distortion and ensure that the spatial modes of the two beams fully coincide.
The total noise level of the electronic link must be controlled at the nV/√Hz level to ensure it does not mask the weak PM-level signals output by the detector.
All components of the environmental isolation system must undergo strict non-magnetic treatment to avoid introducing additional electromagnetic interference under strong electric field loads.
The measured displacement noise of the entire system must reach below 0.02 pm/√Hz, meeting the detection requirements for the limit of particulate noise.
