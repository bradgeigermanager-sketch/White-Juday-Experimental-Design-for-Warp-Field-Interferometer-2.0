🛡️ Core details of full-link electromagnetic shielding wiring<br>
<br>
This cabling scheme is designed for detecting weak signals at the PM level, strictly following the principle of "Faraday cage fully enclosed + complete isolation of strong and weak currents + single-point low-resistance grounding," with a full-link shielding efficiency of ≥80dB, completely preventing power frequency and RF electromagnetic interference from entering signal circuits.<br>

🔌 Cable selection and shielding layer handling details<br>
All weak-signal analog cables use double-layer shielded ZR-KVVRP flame-retardant shielded twisted pair cables, with an inner layer of aluminum foil shielding + outer layer of tin-plated copper mesh braided shielding, covering ≥95% of the coverage density, completely blocking electric field coupling interference.<br>
The high-voltage modulation cable uses a high-magnetic permeability slope-moalloy shielded cable, with a magnetic shielding efficiency of ≥60dB, preventing magnetic field leakage and interference with weak signal circuits during strong electric field loading.<br>
All cable shielding layers are continuous and without breaks throughout. The connectors use a 360° annular crimp shielding structure, eliminating "pig's tail" type of lead grounding and preventing a cliff-like drop in shielding performance at the joints.<br>
The shielding layer of weak signal cables is only connected to signal ground at a single point at the main unit, while the detector end is completely insulated from floating ground, completely cutting off the ground loop and preventing microcurrent interference with weak signals at the nV level caused by ground potential differences.<br>
📏 Details of spatial isolation wiring for strong and weak currents<br>
High-voltage power cables, high-voltage modulation cables, and weak-signal analog cables are fully separated through independent metal cable trays, with a minimum clear distance of ≥300mm laid parallel, fully meeting the isolation requirements for power cables above 380V/2kVA in the GB50312-2016 standard.<br>
If space constraints prevent spacing requirements, a ≥2mm thick grounding metal partition should be installed in the middle of the cable tray, with the strong current side on top and the weak current side below. In this case, the minimum clear distance for parallel laying can be compressed to 80mm.<br>
When strong and weak electrical cables must cross, the crossing angle must be strictly locked at 90°±5°, with a minimum clear distance of ≥50mm at the crossing.<br>
Additional grounding metal shields should be installed on the weak current side, and it is strictly forbidden to increase the length of parallel coupling diagonally across the cable.<br>
All cable routing avoids forming closed loops throughout the process, preventing the power frequency magnetic field from inducing additional interference electromotive force within the loop. The length of weak signal cables is controlled within 3m to minimize "antenna effect."<br>
🏠 Details of the shielding chamber and grounding system<br>
The entire optical breadboard is surrounded by a fully enclosed aluminum Faraday cage, with conductive foam added at all seams. The contact resistance at the seams is ≤ 1mΩ, and the entire cavity shields ≥80dB, completely blocking external RF radiation interference.<br>
Strictly distinguish between protection ground (PE), signal ground (SG), and shielded ground (EG) for independent wiring, completely isolated between the three grounds, with all grounding resistance ≤ 0.5Ω. The grounding terminals are made of gold-plated material to prevent oxidation.<br>
The signal ground backbone uses multi-stranded thick copper wire with a cross-sectional area of ≥16mm², directly connected to an independent grounding pile at a single point from the grounding end of the differential instrument amplifier, without following a star branch throughout the process, eliminating ground loop interference.<br>
All cables passing through the Faraday cage wall are equipped with through-type filters; the power inlet is fitted with π-type low-pass filters, and the signal inlet is fitted with ferrite ferrite rings corresponding to the frequency band; the high-frequency interference suppression ratio is ≥ 100dB.<br>
🔍 Internal compartment division and PCB routing details<br>
The shielding chamber is functionally divided into three independent sub-compartments: high-voltage modulation chamber, weak signal detection chamber, and data acquisition chamber. Grounded metal shielding plates are installed between the sub-chambers to prevent switching noise from high-voltage modules from entering weak signal areas.<br>
The PCB board inside the weak signal detection chamber uses high-TG FR4 boards, with a complete copper foil shielding layer inside. The ground copper sheet thickness is ≥35μm, the ground copper sheet is ≥3mm wide, and the distance between signal ground and power ground is ≥2mm, improving interference suppression rate to 98%.<br>
The signal lines for differential input are run in equal-length differential pairs throughout, with ground wires fully enclosing both sides to further counteract spatial magnetic field coupling interference. The twist of the differential pair is controlled within 5mm, and the common-mode rejection ratio is increased by an order of magnitude.<br>
All digital control cables are individually shielded in cable trays, completely physically isolated from analog weak signal cables to avoid high-speed jump noise from digital circuits polluting analog signal links.<br>
