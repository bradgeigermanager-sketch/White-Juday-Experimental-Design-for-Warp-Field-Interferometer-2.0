📐 Complete hardware wiring of White-Judy Experiment 2.0<br>
<br>
This strictly follows the GB50343-2012 electromagnetic shielding standard, with a full-link shielding efficiency of ≥80dB, fully meeting the anti-interference requirements for detecting weak signals at the PM level:<br>
<br>
High-voltage modulation chamber wiring<br>
<br>
The cable from the 1kHz square wave modulation source to the strong electric field curvature loop uses high-magnetic permeability sloped alloy shielded cables throughout, with a magnetic shielding efficiency of ≥60dB.<br>
The cable runs independently through an independent metal cable tray, laid parallel to weak signal cables, with a clear distance of ≥300mm, fully meeting the isolation requirements for power cables and signal cables in GB50343-2012.<br>
The outer layers of the shielding layer are connected to shielded ground EG at both ends, while the inner layer is only single-point grounded at the modulation source to avoid interference from ground loop current.<br>
<br>
Wiring for weak signal detection pods<br>
<br>
The cables from the two photodetectors to the differential instrument op-amp are all made of double-layer shielded ZR-KVVRP flame-retardant shielded twisted pair cables, with an inner aluminum foil layer + outer tinned copper mesh braided, covering ≥95% of the coverage density.<br>
The inner shielding layer only connects the signal ground SG at a single point at the instrument op-amp end, while the detector end is completely insulated from floating ground, completely cutting off the ground loop and eliminating nV-level microcurrent interference caused by ground potential differences.<br>
The differential signal lines are run in equal-length differential pairs throughout, with ground wires fully enclosing both sides, the twist distance controlled within 5mm, and the common-mode rejection ratio increased to over 10⁷.<br>
<br>
Data acquisition cabin wiring<br>
<br>
All digital control cables are individually shielded in cable trays, completely physically isolated from analog weak signal cables to prevent high-speed digital circuit switching noise from polluting analog signal links.<br>
The grounding of the industrial control host uses two equipotential connection wires, each 0.5m and 0.4m long, differing by 20%, to prevent the ground wire from producing 1/4 wavelength resonance at the interference frequency, which becomes a receiving antenna.<br>
<br>
Wiring is installed through walls in the cross-cabin compartment<br>
<br>
All cables passing through the Faraday cage wall are equipped with through-type filters, with a high-frequency interference suppression ratio of ≥ 100dB.
The shielding layer at the cable crossing the wall is 360° annular crimping with the Faraday cage shell, with no breaks or "pig tail" leads, ensuring shielding performance does not drop sharply.<br>
