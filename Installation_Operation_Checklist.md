🛠️ Step-by-step installation operation checklist<br>
First, place the two-stage active seismic isolation platform in an independent experimental area away from elevators and fans, preheat it up for 30 minutes, and confirm the vibration suppression ratio in the mHz band >10⁶.<br>
ULE ultra-low expansion optical breadboards are laid on the isolation platform, and all optical component mounting bases are made of non-magnetic stainless steel to prevent electromagnetic interference during strong electric field loads.<br>
Fixed ultra-stable He-Ne laser, adjust the laser output height to 150mm, maintain constant height and coaxial throughout the process, ensuring the beam passes through the center of all optical element light holes.<br>
Install the beam splitter and two polarizing prisms in sequence, adjust the beam splitting ratio to a strict 50:50, and keep the initial optical path difference between the two arms within 1cm, ensuring the two beams are completely within the coherent length range.<br>
The experimental beam branch is connected to a 30m compensation delay optical fiber, and the parallel beam branch is installed with an equal-path air optical path. A laser interferometer is used to calibrate the optical path difference between the two arms, with deviation controlled within 2μm.<br>
Two low-noise photodetectors are respectively aligned at the outgoing light spots of the two beams, the detector positions are adjusted so that the spots fully fall at the center of the photosensitive surface, and the photocurrent signal is fed into the two input terminals of the differential instrument op-amp.<br>
All analog signal cables use double-layer shielded coaxial cables, with the shielding layer grounded at one end to prevent power frequency electromagnetic interference from entering weak signal links.<br>
Connected to the full-chamber closed-loop temperature control system, set the target temperature to 25°C, and after waiting 2 hours, confirm that the temperature control accuracy of the entire chamber is stable at 10⁻⁶°C/√Hz.<br>
🔍 Full-process detailed debugging steps<br>
Turn on the ultra-stable He-Ne laser, preheat for 2 hours, confirm light intensity fluctuation <10⁻⁶, and lock in the frequency stabilization system.<br>
Use a beam profiler to calibrate the spot overlap between the two beams, ensuring a centroid deviation of <0.1μm, with perfect spatial modes.<br>
Adjust the common-mode rejection ratio of the differential instrument op-amp, input a common-mode reference signal, and measure the common-mode rejection ratio to >10⁷, confirming that the laser power drift and other common-mode noise are completely canceled.<br>
Start the 1kHz square wave modulation source, synchronously connect the strong electric field curvature loop and the reference terminal of the digital lock-in amplifier, and confirm the phase synchronization deviation of the modulation signal <1ns.<br>
Set the PLO amplifier to a filter bandwidth of 1mHz, gradually adjust the phase knob until the output signal amplitude reaches its maximum value, completing phase locking.<br>
Activate the sampling integrator, set the number of integration cycles to 10,000, and confirm that the signal-to-noise ratio of the output signal is more than 100 times higher than the original signal.<br>
Disconnect the high-voltage power supply from the strong electric field, continuously collect background noise data for 2 hours, calibrate the system's background displacement noise, and confirm that the scattered noise limit of 0.02pm/√Hz is reached.<br>
Connected to a high-precision time-to-digital converter, set the sampling frequency to 1MHz, activate a 72-hour continuous acquisition mode, synchronously record all-dimensional environmental parameters, and complete final state confirmation before the experiment.<br>
